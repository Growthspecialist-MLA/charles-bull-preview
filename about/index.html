About the firm

Meet Charles Bull

A Welsh attorney with decades of public service, focused on helping families protect their retirement from the costs of Alzheimer's and aging.

Schedule a ConsultationCall (337) 329-9243

Charles Bull brings more than 45 years of legal and estate planning experience to every family he works with.

Beyond his practice across Louisiana law, Charles has served the Welsh community in public office for much of his career, as Mayor, as a City Councilman, and as City Magistrate. That experience gives him an unusual view of how legal decisions play out in real families and in a small town.

For almost ten years he also sat on the board of the Welsh nursing home. He saw firsthand the needs and challenges seniors face, and how often families arrive at a crisis without a plan.

His focus: protecting retirement

Charles works with families facing Alzheimer's and aging to create a clear path forward and protect the security they worked hard to build. He enjoys the challenge of finding creative solutions. His goal is to lay out the options available, explain the advantages and consequences of each, and help you choose the path that is best for your family.

Although the office is in Welsh, Charles has helped seniors across Louisiana with wills, trusts, Medicaid planning and successions. When a matter falls outside his practice, he has relationships with trusted attorneys and other professionals and will point you to the right one.
